I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Visual Computing Lab (VCLab) at Sungkyunkwan University, supervised by Prof. Jae-Pil Heo. I received my Master's and Bachelor's degrees from Sungkyunkwan University.

Research

My research focuses on image restoration and generative modeling, with an emphasis on diffusion models and transformer-based vision systems. I am particularly interested in training dynamics and representation quality for high-fidelity image synthesis.
